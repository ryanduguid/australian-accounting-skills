# Source record review, 24 September 2026

This review adds three primary sources to `au-smsf-year-end` for its new
pension minimum and transfer balance account report step. It uses public ATO
and Federal Register of Legislation material and no client data. An agent read
each source on the maintainer's instruction and set `checked_at` to the reading
date; the maintainer may re-date a record after reading it.

## Pension minimum

The [ATO income stream rules page](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/self-managed-super-funds-smsf/paying-smsf-benefits/income-stream-pension-rules-and-payments)
(last updated 30 April 2026) states how the minimum annual payment is worked
out, when it is pro-rated, that no minimum applies to a pension starting on or
after 1 June, and the consequences of missing it.

The [Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Regulations 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B00580/latest/text)
were read as compilation 159 (F2026C00541), in force from 1 July 2026.
Schedule 7 clauses 1 to 5 set the balance and age day, the pro-rating, the
1 June rule and rounding to the nearest $10.

## Transfer balance account reports

The [ATO TBAR page for SMSFs](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/self-managed-super-funds-smsf/smsf-administration-and-reporting/when-to-lodge-a-transfer-balance-account-report-for-smsfs)
(last updated 1 April 2025) lists the events to report and their timing. Its
"When to report" section sets a quarterly timeframe for most events, and its
"Exceeding the personal transfer balance cap" section sets separate timeframes
for a voluntary commutation after an excess transfer balance determination and
for a response to a commutation authority. The skill names both cases without
stating either period.

No record adopts a factor, amount or due date. The skill sends the preparer to
the source for the work's period.
