# Source review: NSW construction and contractor obligations

Review date: 15 August 2026

## Scope and decision boundary

This review closes the identified primary-source gaps in:

- `progress-claim-preparation`, for the NSW mineral-extraction exclusion and
  *Cadia Holdings Pty Ltd v Downer EDI Mining Pty Ltd* [2020] NSWSC 1588
- `retention-schedule`, for the NSW retention-money trust head power,
  current Regulation and regulator guidance
- `contractor-super-tpar`, for SGAA s 12(3), payday super, TPAR and no-ABN
  withholding

It verifies the workflow statements listed below. It is not a contract coverage
opinion, employee-status opinion, authority to withdraw trust money, withholding
decision or lodgment instruction. Those decisions remain with a construction
lawyer, registered tax agent or other qualified reviewer as appropriate.

## Controlling source order

1. Current in-force legislation and regulations
2. Authoritative court judgment text
3. Current regulator rulings and guidance
4. Commentary only as a discovery aid

If a regulator summary conflicts with an in-force instrument, preserve both
citations in the workpaper, apply the instrument as the controlling source and
escalate any consequential action. Do not silently choose the longer or easier
deadline.

## NSW security of payment coverage

| Question | Primary source | Verified result |
|---|---|---|
| What does the mining exclusion say? | [Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 1999 (NSW), s 5](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-1999-046) | Section 5(2)(b) excludes extraction of minerals, including tunnelling, boring or underground works, **for that purpose**. It does not create a site-wide mining exclusion. |
| How was the purpose connection treated in *Cadia*? | [Official NSW Case Law judgment](https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au/decision/175b4bf54ee486b38a0338e5), especially [7]-[8] and [101]-[112] | The Court preferred a narrow connection to actual extraction and held the exclusion was not engaged on the facts before it. Preparatory or associated work was not automatically work for extraction. |
| Can mixed work still produce a construction contract? | Same judgment, especially [147]-[150] | The Court identified haulage, establishment and disestablishment, clean-up, stockpiling and dewatering work capable of falling within s 5(1). Some excluded work did not automatically decide the character of all work under the contract. |

The skill therefore records each activity, its purpose and the contract
structure, but never transfers the outcome in *Cadia* to another contract. A
construction lawyer makes the coverage call.

## NSW retention-money trusts

| Question | Primary source | Verified result |
|---|---|---|
| Is s 12A the head power? | [Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 1999 (NSW), s 12A](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-1999-046) | Yes. It permits regulations requiring retention money held by a head contractor for a subcontractor to be held in trust and paid into a trust account. |
| What currently implements the trust? | [Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Regulation 2020 (NSW), Part 2](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/sl-2020-0504) | Clauses 5-16 govern the trigger, approved ADI, deposits, account, withdrawals, interest, protection from head-contractor debts, overdrafts, closure and records. |
| What triggers the requirement? | Regulation cl 6 | The current clause tests the value of the head contractor's main contract with the principal. If variations take it to the stated threshold later, only subcontracts entered after that point are caught. The current amount must be read from the in-force clause when the skill is used. |
| When must retained money be deposited? | Regulation cl 8 | As soon as possible and no later than 5 business days after the head contractor is required to retain it. |
| When is a new account notified to the Secretary? | Regulation cl 9(3) | Within 10 business days after the account is opened. |
| What is the ledger-statement cadence? | Regulation cl 16 | At least every 3 months, unless a written agreement changes the interval, which cannot exceed 6 months. Required records continue for 3 years after account closure. |
| Is there a continuing annual account-review obligation? | Regulation Part 2 and cl 20A | No continuing annual review was located in the current operative provisions. Clause 20A is a one-off transitional requirement for the financial year commencing 1 July 2019. |
| What withdrawals are allowed? | Regulation cls 10 and 12 | Only the four cl 10 grounds, by cheque or electronic funds transfer. Trust money is not available for the head contractor's debts. |

[NSW Government retention-money guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/compliance-and-regulation/security-of-payment/retention-money)
was also checked. It is useful secondary guidance, but on the review date it
said the Secretary notice was due within 14 days. That conflicts with the
10-business-day rule in cl 9(3). The skill records the conflict and gives the
in-force Regulation precedence.

## Contractor superannuation and reporting

| Question | Primary source | Verified result |
|---|---|---|
| What is the current employee ruling and SGR 2005/1 history? | [TR 2023/4, *Income tax and superannuation guarantee: who is an employee?*](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=TXR/TR20234/NAT/ATO/00001), especially [5A] and Appendix 2 | SGR 2005/1 was withdrawn with effect from 26 June 2024. The Commissioner's continuing views were incorporated into Appendix 2 of the retitled consolidated ruling. |
| What are the s 12(3) elements? | [Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992, s 12](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04402/latest/text) and TR 2023/4 Appendix 2 [93]-[114] | There must be a contract, it must be wholly or principally for a person's labour, and the person must work under it. The bargain and benefits received are assessed from the engaging entity's perspective. |
| Does an interposed entity settle s 12(3)? | [SGR 2005/2](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=SGR/SGR20052/NAT/ATO/00001), especially [10]-[17], read with TR 2023/4 | A genuine contract only with an interposed company, trust or partnership generally does not make the end user the individual's employer under s 12(3). A multi-party document can still contain a bilateral exchange with the individual, so the actual parties and obligations must be traced. Ordinary employment under s 12(1) remains a separate test. |
| Is plant value a percentage test? | TR 2023/4, especially [101] and [108]-[110] | No. Comparable plant and labour values are evidence about the whole bargain and benefits received; no mechanical percentage determines what is principal. |
| What domestic-work exclusion applies? | SGAA s 12(11) | Work wholly or principally of a domestic nature for not more than 30 hours per week is excluded by that subsection. |
| What changed for payday super? | SGAA s 10A and [Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025A00057/latest/text), including item 182 | Payday super commenced on 1 July 2026. For qualifying earnings days on or after that date, s 10A(1)(d) includes payments under an s 12(3) contract in respect of the person's labour. The transition can reach later payments relating to earlier work while preserving the old regime for quarters ending before commencement. |
| How is the bounded post-commencement SG amount calculated? | SGAA ss 10A(5)-(6) and 17A-17D | The maximum contributions base is the current basic concessional contributions cap multiplied by 100 divided by the 12% charge percentage and rounded down to the nearest $10. Section 10A(6) applies the base to each payment in actual payment order using cumulative qualifying earnings for the employee in relation to each employer for the financial year. Under s 17A(2), the adjusted amounts of two or more payments on the same QE day are summed and that QE-day total is multiplied once by 12%. A s 17B certificate in force for the employee in relation to the employer and a period including the QE day instead treats the base as already reached and the individual amount as nil; the s 17D notice and enclosed certificate copy provide the employer and period evidence. |
| When can contributions be applied to that amount? | SGAA ss 18A-18C | Section 18C(1) requires prior-use, fund-receipt-order and nil-floor controls. Its standard periods are the usual period ending on the seventh business day after the QE day and the 12-month period ending the day before that QE day. Section 18C(2) item 1 uses the extended usual period ending on the 20th business day for the specified first-contribution circumstances; item 2 uses the usual period for the first later standard QE day for qualifying earnings determined as out-of-cycle under s 18C(3); item 3 uses the later of the extended usual period and 20 business days starting after an exceptional-circumstances determination under s 18C(4); and item 4 preserves the applicable latest due day from an earlier contribution. |
| Does the skill calculate final SG charge? | SGAA ss 18D-20D | No. It stops after the individual s 17A amount and s 18C contribution-period screen. A registered agent must review the final shortfall, notional earnings, administrative uplift, choice loading and charge. |
| Where does building-and-construction TPAR come from? | [Taxation Administration Act 1953](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1953A00001/latest/text), Schedule 1 Division 405, and [Taxation Administration Regulations 2017, reg 70](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2017L01227/latest/text) | Division 405 authorises reports for supplies specified by regulation; reg 70 specifies building and construction services and its purchaser tests. The former skill statement that construction was not in the Act at all was incorrect. |
| What purchaser tests does reg 70 use? | Regulation 70 | At least half of current-year business activity, current-year income or prior-year income must relate to or derive from building and construction services. Meeting any one of the three current tests is enough. |
| Are materials-only supplies reportable under reg 70? | Regulation 70 | No. The provision covers services or goods and services together, unless the services are merely incidental to the goods. The approved form supplies fields; it does not decide this statutory coverage point. |
| How do the other TPAR industries arise? | TAA Schedule 1 s 396-55 table items 11-14 | Cleaning, courier and road freight, security/investigation/surveillance and IT services are listed in the Act. The consolidated or MEC group and Division 12 withholding exclusions must be applied before reporting. |
| What 10% exemption applies to those industries? | [Taxable Payments Reporting System - Reporting Exemptions for Certain Entities Determination 2019 (F2019L00864)](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2019L00864/latest/text), s 5 | For each item 11-14 service category, an entity with an ABN is exempt only if relevant service consideration received is less than 10% of relevant GST turnover, the transaction is not described in another s 396-55 item, and the entity has not opted in before the due or further allowed time. Current GST turnover applies after at least 12 months of supplies; otherwise projected GST turnover applies. Giving a report is sufficient evidence of choosing to report. This exemption does not apply to Division 405. |
| What is the no-ABN pathway? | TAA Schedule 1 s 12-190 and Taxation Administration Regulations reg 38 | Section 12-190 starts with an Australian-enterprise supply and contains the exceptions. Regulation 38 derives the rate from the current top rate and Medicare levy rate; the skill must calculate it from current sources. |
| What time rule applies to s 396-55? | TAA Schedule 1 s 396-55 and s 388-55 | The defaults are a financial-year reporting period and the 31st day after the period. Another period or time requires the applicable legislative instrument. Section 388-55 is a separate general approved-form power that can defer this lodgment time. |
| What time rule applies to Division 405? | TAA Schedule 1 ss 388-55 and 405-10(1), (2) and (4) | The statutory default is quarterly reporting within 21 days after quarter end. The Commissioner may vary the requirements for a purchaser or class only by written notice under s 405-10(4). Because the report is in the approved form under s 405-10(2), s 388-55 can separately defer its lodgment time. |
| Why is the current path annual and due 28 August? | [PS LA 2011/15, *Lodgment obligations, due dates and deferrals*](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=PSR/PS201115/NAT/ATO/00001), [272]-[274], and [ATO TPRS guidance](https://softwaredevelopers.ato.gov.au/TPRS) | The practice statement records the current Division 405 annual-TPAR administrative path and the 28 August TPAR due date. The applicable s 405-10(4) written notice remains the operative variation evidence, and any s 388-55 deferral is separate; the practice statement is neither. |

SGR 2005/2 remained operative with a draft-replacement status banner on the
review date. SGR 2026/D1 was therefore treated as preliminary, not as a final
ruling. The skill requires a status check each time intermediary arrangements
are analysed.

The automated source gates require:

- official NSW Act, Regulation and Case Law links in the affected skills
- the *Cadia* paragraph ranges and express construction-lawyer escalation

- the SGAA per-employer maximum contributions base, same-day payment aggregation,
  s 17B certificate treatment, the four distinct s 18C longer-period rules and
  the express final-charge boundary
- the F2019L00864 10% exemption conditions and its separation from Division 405
- TAA Division 405 and regulation 70 rather than the former false construction
  pathway, with the quarterly default, written-notice variation and current
  annual 28 August path kept distinct from s 396-55, while the general s 388-55
  approved-form deferral power remains available to either reporting pathway
- preservation of human review, no-withdrawal, no-lodgment and no-client-data
  boundaries
