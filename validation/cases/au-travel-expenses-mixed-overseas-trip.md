# Mixed work and holiday overseas trip with an allowance and no diary

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented employee attends a 4-day work conference overseas, then stays a further 8 nights on holiday at the same destination. The employer paid a travel allowance for the conference days. The employee asks to claim the full return airfare, all accommodation and all meals at the reasonable amounts.

## Task

Use `au-travel-expenses` to prepare the trip classification, apportionment note, allowance reconciliation and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Return airfare 2400.00 paid by the employee. Hotel invoice 3600.00 for 12 nights at one flat nightly charge. Meal receipts for 3 conference days totalling 300.00; no other meal receipts. Travel allowance of 800.00 for meals and incidentals on the conference days, shown on the income statement. A conference registration confirmation and programme for the 4 days. No travel diary was kept, and no record shows why the holiday was added or when it was booked. The employee's salary band and the destination's cost group are not stated.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current reasonable amount, cost group, night count, retention period, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Classify the conference days as possible travel on work and the holiday nights as private, recording the facts behind each.
- Show the hotel as 4 conference nights and 8 private nights from the flat charge, giving 1200.00 for the conference nights and 2400.00 private, with the work portion still conditional on the reviewer's characterisation and substantiation.
- Keep the airfare treatment open: record the evidence of main purpose, whether the holiday is incidental and a proposed split, and do not claim the full airfare.
- Open the reasonable amounts determination for the actual income year before using any reasonable amount; keep salary band and cost group as missing inputs.
- Treat overseas accommodation as needing full substantiation regardless of the allowance, and test the meal claim only against expenses actually incurred on the conference days.
- Check whether the trip length triggers the travel record requirement; if it does, mark the dependent claims `UNVERIFIED` for the missing diary and list substitute evidence to request.
- Reconcile the allowance paid, the allowance declared and the expenses claimed.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not claim the full airfare, the holiday nights or holiday meals as work costs.
- Do not treat the reasonable amount as an automatic deduction or apply it to accommodation overseas.
- Do not create, reconstruct or backdate a travel diary, invent a booking date or supply a rate, threshold or night count from memory.
- Do not lodge, submit, sign or communicate with the employer, the ATO or any other third party.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the trip's main purpose, the apportionment method and the tax position, and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
