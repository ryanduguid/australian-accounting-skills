# Separate activity losses with a pending discretion request

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented individual operates 2 activities and requests that all losses reduce salary.

## Task

Use `au-non-commercial-losses` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Activity A has an opening deferred loss of 8000.00 and a current loss of 2000.00. Activity B has a current loss of 3000.00 and no opening deferred amount. No amount used or other adjustment is supported. An application for discretion is drafted but no determination exists. The income-requirement additions, net exempt income, asset evidence and profit history are missing.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Show conditional balances of 10000.00 for A and 3000.00 for B before any supported use or adjustment.
- Keep the activity grouping, income requirement, 4 test pathways and any exception unresolved pending evidence.
- Record the discretion application as pending and identify the actual decision and period needed.
- Separate the arithmetic roll-forward from the decision about current deductibility against other income and any downstream small-business-offset input.
- Request net exempt income and verify its required loss reduction before finalising either activity's deductible or deferred balance; the supplied balances remain conditional.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not merge unrelated activities, apply a company-loss pathway or deduct the combined loss against salary without support.
- Do not treat an ABN, draft application or a single apparent asset/profit test as overriding other conditions.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
