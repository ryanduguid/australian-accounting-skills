# Business offset with unresolved PSI and deferred losses

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented individual asks for the unincorporated small business income tax offset.

## Task

Use `au-small-business-income-offset` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The supplied ordinary-business schedule has income of 60000.00 and deductions of 15000.00. A separate contractor stream has receipts of 20000.00 with PSI/PSB treatment unreviewed. A separate opening deferred Division 35 loss is 5000.00 and no current use is supported. A trust statement shows a 10000.00 cash distribution but no net-small-business-income component. Aggregated-turnover and whole-return tax inputs are missing.

For separate branch checks, an individual receives 8000.00 through a non-operating trust that only receives another entity's business distribution. In another independent example, sole-trader activities show profit of 45000.00 and a currently deductible loss of 5000.00; a separate partnership share is 2000.00 before allowable individual partner deductions of 3000.00. These are supplied classification assumptions for conditional arithmetic, not verified statutory treatment.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Reconcile the supported ordinary-business subtotal to 45000.00 without claiming it is the final statutory net-small-business-income amount.
- Keep the contractor stream conditional on PSI/PSB and the deferred loss separate pending supported use; do not deduct it automatically.
- Do not substitute the trust cash distribution for its required statutory income component.
- Require the distributing entity's own-business evidence and keep the 8000.00 interposed-trust amount outside the proposed eligible bridge unless applicable authority supports inclusion.
- For the separate netting example, show conditional combined sole-trader income of 40000.00 and a partnership share floored at zero after individual deductions, rather than 39000.00 after cross-source negative netting. Verify the assumed loss/deduction treatment before adopting either amount.
- Identify eligibility, aggregation, whole-return taxable-income/basic-tax and year-specific rate/cap inputs required before the offset calculation.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not calculate the offset from turnover or cash, apply company-tax relief to the individual or assume every PSI amount is eligible.
- Do not claim a final offset or refund from the 45000.00 subtotal alone.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
