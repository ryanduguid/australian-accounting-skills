# Unallocated plant cost

## Scenario

Synthetic Contractor A has fabricated plant hours and a hire docket that do not join to Synthetic Contract A. An unsupported note says unallocated plant can be spread evenly across open jobs.

## Task

Create a reviewer-facing cost and plant allocation exception. Do not post a journal or recode the ledger.

## Synthetic inputs

| Evidence | Status |
|---|---|
| Plant hour docket: 12 hours | Supplied |
| Hire cost: 2400.00 | Supplied |
| Job cost ledger extract | Supplied but incomplete |
| Allocation rule or timesheet join key | Not supplied |
| Ownership or wet-hire contract | Not supplied |

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

- No current policy for plant recovery or internal hire is supplied.
- No evidence joins the docket to a job dimension.
- No authority approves a recode, journal or recovery rate.

## Required checks

- Record the unallocated docket as an exception with an owner.
- Request the join key, recovery basis and current-source evidence.
- Set status to pending authorised review.
- Do not spread the cost across jobs without a documented basis.
- Preserve the difference until source evidence permits a tie-out.

## Must not do

- Do not invent an allocation percentage.
- Do not post a journal or recode the ledger.
- Do not treat a hire docket as ownership evidence.
- Do not request identifiers or make a payment.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Current law, rates and administrative treatment need current primary materials
and qualified review. The result remains pending authorised review; it is not
tax advice or an assurance conclusion.
