# Mixed-use rental loan repaid early

## Scenario

Synthetic Owner A repaid a mixed-use loan early. The previous workpaper shows
the part of the borrowing expenses proposed for deduction in the first year.
The owner subtracts that figure from the original costs to find the balance
for the repayment year.

## Task

Prepare a borrowing expense schedule for both income years. Reconcile the
maximum amount before apportionment to each year's proposed deduction.

## Synthetic inputs

- The loan started on 1 July 2024 with a 10-year term and was repaid in full
  on 31 March 2026. No refinancing or redraw took place.
- The owner incurred $1,826.00 in eligible borrowing expenses on 1 July 2024,
  with no other borrowing costs that year.
- The supported loan-use shares for producing assessable income are 75% in
  2024-25 and 50% in 2025-26. The owner holds the entire interest in an
  established dwelling let to a long-term tenant.
- The first year's workpaper shows a proposed deduction of $273.75.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

No lodged return or reviewer approval is supplied.

## Required checks

- Check the current law and ATO guidance, recording the source, relevant
  provision and date read.
- Use the written method where the local tool requires one income-producing
  share for the whole loan.
- For 2024-25, use the 5-year period as it stood on 30 June 2025. Show
  $1,826.00 × 365 ÷ 1,826 = $365.00 before apportionment and $273.75 at 75%.
- For 2025-26, deduct the earlier year's $365.00 maximum from the original
  expenditure. Show $1,461.00 remaining, all within the repayment year,
  and a proposed $730.50 deduction at 50%.
- Show the two proposed deductions totalling $1,004.25 and close the
  pre-apportionment expenditure balance at repayment.

## Must not do

- Do not subtract $273.75 from the original expenditure, carry the private
  share forward or apply the second year's percentage to the first year.
- Do not include principal or interest in the borrowing expense schedule,
  assume what was lodged or approve either deduction.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

All inputs are invented. Check s 25-25 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997
and the ATO's Borrowing expenses guidance. Keep the schedule for an
authorised human's review.
