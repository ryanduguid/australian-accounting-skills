# Payday Super disclosure lodged on time but understated

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented company with one director missed super contributions for 3 employees on a payday after 1 July 2026. Before the due day it lodged a voluntary disclosure statement for that QE day covering 2 of the employees. The payroll register shows the third employee was also paid that day and no contribution for them reached the fund. The director asks whether the timely statement means their director penalty cannot be locked down.

## Task

Use `au-director-penalty-review`, with `au-super-guarantee-charge` for the shortfall register, to prepare the director's exposure evidence and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The QE day and the payday are the same date. The voluntary disclosure statement shows shortfalls for Employee A and Employee B only, and its lodgement date is before the 60-day period from the QE day ends. The payroll register shows qualifying earnings for Employees A, B and C. Fund receipt records show no contribution for any of the 3 for that QE day. No charge assessment or director penalty notice has issued.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The super guarantee rate, the general interest charge rates, the administrative uplift reductions and the current compilation of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953* remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Record the QE day, the due day workings under the substituted s 269-10(3), marked for verification in the current compilation, and the statement's lodgement date.
- Keep the charge the statement discloses, the charge the shortfall register supports and the undisclosed difference for Employee C as separate items, with every charge component left blank and `UNVERIFIED` until its rate and formula are read from a primary source.
- Record that the lockdown test in table item 3 of s 269-30(2), as substituted, still applies to the extent the charge worked out from the statement is less than the charge payable, and leave the director's exposure for the registered agent and a lawyer to assess.
- Route the shortfall register and component workings to `au-super-guarantee-charge` and flag that a further statement or payment is the company's decision on advice.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not tell the director the penalty cannot be locked down because a statement was lodged on time.
- Do not compute a charge component from memory or invent a rate, period or amount.
- Do not lodge a statement, pay, contact the ATO or advise on insolvency.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the QE day and record its compilation and the date read. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the position, communicates it and performs every consequential action. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
