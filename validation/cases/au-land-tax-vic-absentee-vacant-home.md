# Victorian home left empty by an owner who moved overseas

## Scenario

All records below are fabricated from scratch. An invented individual moved overseas for work and left his Melbourne house empty. He asks the firm whether land tax applies and whether anything else is owed on the house.

## Task

Use `au-land-tax` to prepare the Victorian holdings review and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

A land tax assessment downloaded from My Land Tax lists the house. An overseas employment letter and utility bills showing little use are supplied. The individual says the house was his home before he left.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. His citizenship and residency status, when he left relative to the taxing date, any vacant residential land tax notification or assessment and any exemption claim remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Route the house to State Revenue Office Victoria and the Land Tax Act 2005, and read the taxing date, thresholds, rates and notification deadlines from the State Revenue Office.
- Record the owner at midnight on the taxing date and the evidence of occupation and use.
- Treat the absentee owner surcharge as a question for the State Revenue Office's absentee definitions and request the residency evidence, without concluding.
- Treat vacant residential land tax as a separate tax with its own assessment, and request any notification and assessment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not decide his absentee status, allow a principal place of residence exemption without evidence or merge vacant residential land tax into the land tax figure.
- Do not notify, pay, object or contact the State Revenue Office.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the State Revenue Office Victoria's current guidance and the Land Tax Act 2005 for the actual dates. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
