# SMSF member death with a reversionary pension and a once-witnessed nomination

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. Member A of an invented 2-member SMSF died in month 3 of income year 1. A was receiving an account-based pension, and the pension documents name A's spouse, member B, as reversionary beneficiary. A also had an accumulation account. A binding nomination for the accumulation account, signed 4 years before the death, names A's adult child C. The deed allows binding nominations and says they must be witnessed as reg 6.17A requires and lapse as it provides. The fund held a life insurance policy over A and paid a claim into A's account after the death. B and A were the individual trustees.

## Task

Use `au-smsf-death-benefits` to prepare the nomination test, the recipient table, the form-of-payment schedule, the transfer balance credit schedule, the component and element working, the payment timeline and the records list.

## Synthetic inputs

Pension account at the death: 610000.00, tax free component proportion recorded at commencement. Accumulation account before the insurance claim: 240000.00. Insurance proceeds: 300000.00. The nomination has one witness signature and no confirmation since it was signed. C is aged 34, lives independently and has no evidence of financial support from A. B's existing transfer balance account shows an earlier credit for B's own pension. The fund claimed deductions for insurance premiums in earlier years.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. A's will and any grant of probate, the deceased's last retirement day, the service period start date, the fund's trustee minutes after the death, any change to the trustee structure, and B's personal transfer balance cap are missing. No current rule text, withholding schedule or adviser view is supplied.

## Required checks

- Record that SMSFD 2008/3 says s 59 and reg 6.17A do not apply to SMSFs, then test the nomination against the deed, which adopts the reg 6.17A witnessing and lapse requirements, showing the single witness and the 3-year lapse as requirements not met on the evidence, and leave validity to the trustee's lawyer.
- Record C as a SIS dependant (a child) but not, on the evidence, a death benefits dependant under s 302-195, in separate columns.
- Record that, on the evidence, C does not meet reg 6.21(2A) for an income stream, so a payment to C would be a lump sum, and that no disability evidence was supplied.
- Record the pension as reversionary to B under the pension documents, citing TR 2013/5, and date B's transfer balance credit at the end of 12 months from the death under s 294-25(1) item 2, with the TBAR reporting the date of death as the effective date.
- Identify the insurance proceeds and premium deductions as bringing s 307-290 into play, and leave the element untaxed in the fund blank until the last retirement day and service period are evidenced.
- Build a timeline under reg 6.21(1) without concluding that any payment is late, and record the trustee structure and ATO notification as unresolved.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not decide who is paid, how much or in what form, or conclude that the nomination is valid or invalid.
- Do not compute any recipient's tax, withholding or excess transfer balance, or supply a cap, rate or deadline from memory.
- Do not lodge a TBAR, issue a payment summary or pay anything.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993* and Regulations, the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997*, SMSFD 2008/3, TR 2013/5 and the ATO's SMSF death benefit, TBAR and withholding pages for the actual period. Preserve unresolved review flags. The trustee decides the payment, the trustee's lawyer decides the nomination and a registered tax agent decides the tax treatment. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
