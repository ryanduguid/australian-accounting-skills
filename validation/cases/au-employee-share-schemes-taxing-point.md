# ESS sale with missing scheme and restriction evidence

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented employee supplies a broker sale and an employer ESS statement that need reconciliation.

## Task

Use `au-employee-share-schemes` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

One grant records 100 interests, 20 forfeitures and a broker sale of 80 shares for 6400.00 with a 40.00 fee. The employer statement shows a 3000.00 discount but gives a different event date from the broker. The grant agreement, scheme category, restriction-release date and valuation are missing. The employee changed residence during the grant period.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Reconcile 100 less 20 to the 80 sold and bridge gross proceeds of 6400.00 to net cash of 6360.00.
- Retain both dates and the statement discount as evidence to check rather than selecting the convenient date.
- Identify the acquisition regime, scheme and taxing-point conditions, disposal-window test and service/residency allocation that require authority or records.
- Keep the ESS inclusion separate from a later disposal and leave its cost base, acquisition date and taxable gain unresolved.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not treat vesting, cessation or sale automatically as the ESS taxing point.
- Do not calculate CGT using a nil or invented basis or assert that prior ESS taxation exempts a sale.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
