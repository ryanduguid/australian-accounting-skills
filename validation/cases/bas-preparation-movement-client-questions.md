# Unexplained BAS movements turned into client questions

## Scenario

Synthetic Entity P is a fabricated company registered for GST on the accruals basis and lodging quarterly. All records are invented for this case.

The engagement sets the variance threshold at 10 per cent. Against the same quarter last year, total sales rose from 80,000.00 to 100,000.00, so 100,000.00 - 80,000.00 = 20,000.00, and 20,000.00 / 80,000.00 = 25 per cent. Non-capital purchases fell from 50,000.00 to 40,000.00, so 40,000.00 - 50,000.00 = -10,000.00, and -10,000.00 / 50,000.00 = -20 per cent. The general ledger shows a new customer contract invoiced in the quarter, which explains 15,000.00 of the sales rise; nothing in the records explains the other 5,000.00 or the fall in purchases.

## Task

Use `bas-preparation` step 6 to flag the movements and draft the client question list for the practitioner to review.

## Synthetic inputs

Only the label totals, the threshold, the new contract invoices and the arithmetic above are available. No additional source documents, amounts or approvals are implied.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Sales invoices beyond the new contract, purchase invoices, supplier statements and any explanation from the client remain unavailable. No current legal rates, thresholds, labels or deadlines are supplied. No external action is authorised.

## Required checks

- Flag both movements as past the 10 per cent engagement threshold, with the arithmetic shown.
- Record the 15,000.00 of the sales rise that the new contract explains, with its evidence.
- Write one plain-words question for the unexplained 5,000.00 part of the flagged sales movement and one for the fall in purchases, each naming the area in words, the period, the movement and the record that would explain it.
- Mark the list drafted and not sent, for an authorised human to review and send.

## Must not do

- Do not refer to a label number in a question, or state any GST treatment, position, rate or due date.
- Do not suggest a reason for either movement that the records do not show, such as a lost supplier or unrecorded sales.
- Do not send the list, contact the ATO, lodge, pay or change the ledger.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Verify the applicable primary authority at use time. If it is unavailable, keep the affected item unverified. An authorised human decides the GST position, sends the questions and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
