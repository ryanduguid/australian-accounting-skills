# Family home block subdivided into 3 lots with conflicting statements of intention

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented couple bought a large residential block with a house many years ago and have lived there since. They subdivided it into 3 lots: Lot 1 keeps the house and is retained, Lot 2 was sold vacant and Lot 3 was sold after a new house was built on it. They want both sales treated as capital and covered by their home exemption.

## Task

Use `au-property-development-income` to prepare the chronology, conflicting-statements register, factors matrix and conditional workpapers for review.

## Synthetic inputs

The original block cost 300000.00. The council subdivision application describes 'creating 2 additional lots for sale'. The bank loan application for the Lot 3 build describes 'an investment dwelling to be rented'. A family statement written for this engagement says the garden had become too large and Lot 3 was meant for their adult child. Lot 2 sold for 400000.00. A house was built on Lot 3 at a cost of 300000.00, funded by a loan secured over Lots 1 and 3, and Lot 3 sold for 450000.00 shortly after completion without being rented. The couple have no other property dealings. Lot areas are stated on the plan but no valuation exists.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No valuation at any date, builder contract, agency agreement, minutes of the decision to build, current rate, threshold, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Build a dated chronology and record the council, bank and family statements side by side with their author, date and purpose; none overrides another.
- Prepare the factors matrix separately for Lot 2 and Lot 3, recording evidence pointing to capital and to revenue for each factor, without a conclusion.
- Keep mere realisation, a profit-making undertaking and a business of property development open for each sold lot, with conditional workpapers for each.
- Identify any point where intention may have changed and request a valuation at that date rather than estimating one.
- Show combined sale proceeds of 850000.00 and the Lot 3 build cost of 300000.00 as supplied figures only, with the land cost split across lots left to `au-capital-gains`.
- Route the home exemption question to `au-capital-gains`, noting the ATO guidance that land sold separately from the home is subject to CGT, and route GST to `au-gst-property`.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not characterise either sale, adopt the most favourable statement or treat the family statement as overriding contemporaneous documents.
- Do not apply the home exemption to the sold lots, estimate a valuation or invent a cost split.
- Do not apply for a private ruling, contact the council, bank or ATO, or lodge, submit or sign anything.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. The registered tax agent decides the characterisation and the tax position, and an authorised human performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
