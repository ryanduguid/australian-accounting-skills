# Northern Territory business sale resting on an undated option

## Scenario

All records below are fabricated from scratch. An invented buyer is purchasing a Darwin business under a conveyance executed in 2026. The conveyance recites an earlier option to purchase, and the buyer's accountant assumes goodwill is outside duty.

## Task

Use `au-transfer-duty` to prepare the transaction and evidence matrix and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The conveyance allocates a total price of 340000.00: plant and equipment 90000.00, chattels 20000.00, a lease of the premises 30000.00, goodwill 180000.00 and a business name 20000.00. The option deed is not supplied and nothing states when the option was granted or what property it covered. Every asset is in the Northern Territory.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The option deed, the transaction's duty rates, the purchaser's status, any concession or exemption and independent valuations remain missing. No legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Route the transaction to the Territory Revenue Office, the Northern Territory Government overview of dutiable property and s 116 of the Stamp Duty Act 1978.
- Show that the answer turns on the option: under s 116(1) the provisions in force immediately before 9 May 2023 apply where a person entered into an agreement or option before that date to purchase the property or substantially similar property.
- Show both branches for the goodwill and business name without choosing one: not dutiable for a conveyance outside s 116, and within the former definition of dutiable property for one that s 116 covers.
- Show the plant and equipment as dutiable only if acquired with other dutiable property, and the chattels as subject to the exception for a lease of nil or nominal value, with the lease's value still needing evidence.
- Request the option deed as the first unresolved item.
- Leave duty, the dutiable value and any rate uncalculated unless a period-specific rule is verified; otherwise mark them `UNVERIFIED`.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not assume the option's date or conclude that goodwill is outside duty.
- Do not state a rate, threshold or concession from memory.
- Do not decide liability, submit an assessment, declare, pay or contact the revenue office.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the revenue office's current page and the jurisdiction's Act for the transaction date. Preserve unresolved review flags. The conveyancer or lawyer decides liability and performs declarations, assessment submissions and payment. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
