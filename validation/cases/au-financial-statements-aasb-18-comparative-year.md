# A for-profit Tier 1 year before AASB 18 applies

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. Synthetic Entity B,
a for-profit entity preparing Tier 1 general purpose financial statements, asks
for its draft statements pack for the year ending 31 December 2026. Its
reviewer has noted that 'a new presentation standard starts soon' but has not
recorded which standard, when it applies or what these statements must say
about it.

## Task

Use `au-financial-statements` to prepare the step 1 framework and
effective-standards record and the disclosure-checklist items that follow
from it.

## Synthetic inputs

- Period: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026; annual; AUD.
- Entity class: for-profit and not a superannuation entity. The reviewer has
  recorded and approved a Tier 1 general purpose framework.
- The prior year's statements used AASB 101 formats. The draft notes have no
  paragraph on standards issued but not yet effective.
- No decision to apply any standard early is recorded.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

No assessment of the new standard's impact on the statement of profit or loss,
no AASB pronouncement text and no local corpus configuration are supplied. No
current application date, legal approval or authority to sign or issue
statements is supplied.

## Required checks

- Read AASB 18's application paragraphs C1 and AusC1.1 from a live AASB source
  or the configured corpus before classifying the period, or leave the
  classification `UNVERIFIED`.
- Record the entity class, the first annual period AASB 18 applies to and
  whether the year ending 31 December 2026 is the comparative period
  immediately before it.
- Flag that the first AASB 18 statements will present this year's figures
  retrospectively with a reconciliation of each statement of profit or loss
  line item, citing AASB 18 paragraphs C2 and C3 as read at use time.
- Add a disclosure-checklist item for the AASB 108 paragraph 30 disclosure of
  a standard issued but not yet effective, confirmed in the AASB 108 version
  for the period, and record its impact information as missing evidence.
- Leave any early application to the reviewer.

## Must not do

- Do not state AASB 18's application dates from memory or present these
  statements in AASB 18 formats without a recorded early-application decision.
- Do not invent an impact estimate, certify compliance, sign or issue
  statements, or request real identifiers.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the AASB standards and compilations that apply to the actual period.
Preserve unresolved review flags. A qualified reviewer approves accounting
judgements and an authorised human signs and issues. This is not tax, legal or
financial advice or an assurance conclusion.
