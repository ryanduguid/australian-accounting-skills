# Tasmanian business sale conditional on a new lease

## Scenario

All records below are fabricated from scratch. An invented buyer is purchasing a Tasmanian bicycle shop with no freehold land. The sale is conditional on the landlord granting the buyer a new lease of the premises.

## Task

Use `au-transfer-duty` to prepare the transaction and evidence matrix and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The agreement allocates a total price of 215000.00: plant and equipment 45000.00, stock in trade 60000.00, a registered motor vehicle 15000.00, intellectual property 10000.00, business goodwill 55000.00 and an item labelled only 'goodwill' 30000.00. No depreciation schedule or evidence of duty paid on the vehicle's registration transfer is supplied. Every asset is in Tasmania.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The transaction date's duty rates, the purchaser's status, any concession or exemption and independent valuations remain missing. No legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Route the transaction to State Revenue Office Tasmania's sales of business page and its business assets guideline, and record both with the date read.
- Show that the plant and equipment are not dutiable alone but enter duty here because the transfer is conditional on a new lease of the business premises.
- Show the stock in trade, registered vehicle and intellectual property as the guideline's common non-dutiable assets, and the 55000.00 as business goodwill.
- Keep the 30000.00 'goodwill' unresolved: business goodwill is non-dutiable but site goodwill is not, and the label does not say which.
- Request the depreciation schedule and vehicle evidence the State Revenue Office asks for.
- Leave duty, the dutiable value and any rate uncalculated unless a period-specific rule is verified; otherwise mark them `UNVERIFIED`.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not treat the 30000.00 as business goodwill without evidence.
- Do not state a rate, threshold or concession from memory.
- Do not decide liability, submit an assessment, declare, pay or contact the revenue office.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the revenue office's current page and the jurisdiction's Act for the transaction date. Preserve unresolved review flags. The conveyancer or lawyer decides liability and performs declarations, assessment submissions and payment. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
