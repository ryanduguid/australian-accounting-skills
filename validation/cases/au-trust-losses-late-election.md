# Trust loss after an appointor change and a later family trust election

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented discretionary trust made a tax loss in income year 1. In income year 2 a new appointor replaced the founder and 2 new beneficiaries were added by deed variation. In income year 4 the trustee made a family trust election specifying income year 3. The trustee now wants to deduct the income year 1 loss in income year 4 and also holds a capital loss from income year 1. A related company outside the family group paid the trust a management fee in income year 4.

## Task

Use `au-trust-losses` to prepare the loss register, the trust classification for the test period, the evidence schedule for each applicable test, the income injection screen, the election record and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Tax loss in income year 1: 48000.00. Capital loss in income year 1: 12000.00. Income distributions: income year 0 to individuals A (60 parts), B (40 parts); income year 4 to individuals A (30 parts), C (70 parts), resolved before the end of income year 4. No distributions in income years 1 to 3. The election names individual A and was lodged in income year 4. Deed variation and appointor change documents are supplied; the corporate trustee's share register is not.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The ATO's record of the election, the family control test evidence for income year 3, the corporate trustee's shareholder history and the terms of the management fee arrangement remain missing. No current rule text, Commissioner discretion or adviser view is supplied.

## Required checks

- Keep the capital loss out of the workpaper and record the ATO statement that the trust loss provisions do not apply to capital losses.
- Record the test period from the beginning of income year 1 to the end of income year 4, and that the election does not cover the whole test period.
- Evidence the pattern of distributions, stake and control tests separately, each with its section and a status of met, not met or `UNVERIFIED`.
- List the appointor change and the new beneficiaries as control test facts for the reviewer, without concluding that a group began to control the trust.
- Screen the management fee for income injection, recording that a family trust stays within that test.
- Record the election's specified individual and year, and mark the family control test evidence and ATO record `UNVERIFIED`.
- Leave the deduction amount blank and record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not conclude that the loss can or cannot be deducted, or assume a Commissioner discretion.
- Do not recommend making, varying or revoking an election, or treat the election as covering income years before income year 3.
- Do not supply a test period, rule or share from memory, or lodge anything.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use Schedule 2F to the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1936* and the ATO's trust loss and family trusts pages for the actual years. Preserve unresolved review flags. The reviewer decides whether each loss can be deducted and the trustee decides any election. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
