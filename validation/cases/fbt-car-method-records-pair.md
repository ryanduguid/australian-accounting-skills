# FBT car valuation with and without operating cost records

## Scenario

Synthetic Employer M prepares a draft comparison for 2 cars in the FBT year
ended 31 March 2026. Each is an employer-owned car available for private use
for the whole FBT year. The employer asks to elect the lower method for both.

## Task

Prepare a per-car review schedule showing which draft calculations the records
support and what the reviewer needs before making a method decision.

## Synthetic inputs

| Evidence | Car A | Car B |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase invoice, statutory base value workpaper and availability register | Supplied | Supplied |
| Employee contributions | Nil, confirmed against the ledger | Nil, confirmed against the ledger |
| Operating cost reconciliation, including deemed depreciation and interest workings | Supplied | Same total claimed, with no supporting reconciliation |
| Logbook and opening and closing odometer records | Supplied for review, with a business-use calculation | Business-use percentage typed in an email, with no records |

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

The file has no approved election, and no copy of the authority that applies
to this FBT year. No numerical bases or rates are supplied in the case.

## Required checks

- Separate the statutory formula and operating cost evidence for each car.
  Prepare the statutory formula basis for both cars, pending verification of
  the period's rules and the supplied base value and days.
- For Car A, review the operating cost components, business-use calculation
  and validity of the records before comparing the 2 draft values.
- For Car B, mark operating cost as unsupported and request the reconciliation
  and the log book and odometer records that could substantiate business use.
  Record that ss 10A and 10B of the Fringe Benefits Tax Assessment Act 1986
  allow a business-use reduction to the operating cost only where those records
  are kept, so the email percentage supports none. Keep the supported
  statutory formula preparation available for review.
- Check the period-specific method and election requirements with the ATO
  and leave the choice with the authorised reviewer.

## Must not do

- Do not use Car A's percentage or costs for Car B, treat Car B's unreconciled
  cost total as supported or present an email percentage as an accepted record.
- Do not invent a figure, make an election or prepare a final return.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the ATO's car fringe benefits guidance and the authority it cites for the
FBT year ended 31 March 2026. The authorised reviewer confirms whether the
records and calculation support each method and makes the method decision.
