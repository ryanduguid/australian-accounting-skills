# Early farm management deposit withdrawal with unvalued natural increase

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented individual runs a cattle grazing business. A farm management deposit made early in the year was partly repaid 5 months later. The farmer says the repayment is covered by drought and asks for the full deposit to be deducted and the livestock schedule to be closed from head counts alone.

## Task

Use `au-primary-production` to prepare the deposit register, exception evidence list, livestock reconciliation and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Deposit of 50000.00 with one provider; repayment of 20000.00 5 months after the deposit. Provider statements confirm both amounts and interest of 300.00 paid during the year. The farmer refers to a drought declaration letter but has not supplied it, and no rainfall records or disaster assistance documents are supplied. Off-farm consulting income was earned during the year; its deductions are not supplied. Livestock records show 400 opening head valued at 280000.00 in last year's return on a cost basis, 120 calves born, 90 head sold for 99000.00 and 6 deaths, giving 424 closing head. No natural increase cost election, no closing valuation basis and no ration records are supplied.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current income test, holding limit, minimum amount, prescribed natural increase cost, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Record the deposit and repayment in the register and check whether the repayment falls within the s 393-40 early-repayment period; if it does, keep 20000.00 outside the deduction unless an exception is established. The remaining 30000.00 stays conditional on the owner's eligibility and the year's non-primary production income test.
- List the evidence each possible exception needs, noting that the ATO describes the drought exception by reference to rainfall records; a drought declaration letter, even once supplied, must be checked against that test.
- Keep the off-farm income's taxable amount open because its deductions are missing, and do not complete the income test without it.
- Record the interest of 300.00 for assessability review and the repayment's treatment under s 393-40 or s 393-10.
- Reconcile head counts: 400 opening plus 120 born less 90 sold and 6 deaths equals 424 closing.
- Keep the 120 calves unvalued until the s 70-55 cost election is made and any prescribed cost is verified, and keep the closing value blank until a valuation basis is chosen for the class.
- Confirm the opening value of 280000.00 equals last year's closing value.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not deduct the full deposit, treat the farmer's statement or an unsighted letter as meeting the drought exception, or assume a rainfall result.
- Do not supply a prescribed natural increase cost, income test or limit from memory, or value the calves at nil without a recorded election.
- Do not instruct the deposit provider, amend a return, lodge, submit, sign or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the business, exception, election and valuation positions, and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
