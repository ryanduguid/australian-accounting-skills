# Victorian motel land with goods under a separate contract

## Scenario

All records below are fabricated from scratch. An invented buyer is purchasing a Victorian motel. The land is in one contract and the furniture, laundry equipment and shop stock are in a second contract signed the same day between the same parties, and the seller says the second contract is outside duty because it is separate.

## Task

Use `au-transfer-duty` to prepare the transaction and evidence matrix and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The land contract states 1200000.00, including goodwill the parties value at 100000.00. The second contract states 75000.00: furniture and laundry equipment used in the motel business 70000.00 and goods held for sale in the motel shop 5000.00. Every asset is in Victoria.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The transaction date's duty rates, the purchaser's status, any concession or exemption and independent valuations remain missing. No legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Route the transaction to State Revenue Office Victoria, its transfer of land and business or goods page and s 10(1) of the Duties Act 2000.
- Test the 2 contracts together as an arrangement rather than accepting the seller's statement that the second stands alone.
- Show the furniture and laundry equipment as goods used in connection with a business carried on on the land, and the shop goods as stock-in-trade excluded by s 10(1)(d), subject to evidence of how they are held.
- Record Revenue Ruling DA-029's statement that duty is not charged separately on the sale of a business, and flag for the reviewer that the business may still affect the land's value.
- Record that the State Revenue Office says Revenue Ruling DA-054 is being reviewed, and rest on the Act rather than that ruling alone.
- Leave duty, the dutiable value and any rate uncalculated unless a period-specific rule is verified; otherwise mark them `UNVERIFIED`.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not accept the separate contract as settling the treatment of the goods.
- Do not state a rate, threshold or concession from memory.
- Do not decide liability, submit an assessment, declare, pay or contact the revenue office.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the revenue office's current page and the jurisdiction's Act for the transaction date. Preserve unresolved review flags. The conveyancer or lawyer decides liability and performs declarations, assessment submissions and payment. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
