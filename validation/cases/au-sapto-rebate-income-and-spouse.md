# SAPTO with an incomplete rebate-income bridge

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented retiree asks for SAPTO based on age and taxable income. Spouse eligibility and income have not been established.

## Task

Use `au-sapto` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Taxable income is 30000.00, reportable super contributions are 2000.00 and net investment losses are 1500.00. Reportable fringe benefits are unknown. The spouse lives apart for an unconfirmed reason and period. No pension-eligibility evidence, spouse return or current authority is supplied.

For independent transfer-branch comparisons, the donor is respectively a resident, a foreign resident without an Australian Government pension or allowance, and a foreign resident receiving one. Taxable income is positive in all 3 copies. No year-specific transfer authority or complete eligibility evidence is supplied.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Show the supplied subtotal of 33500.00 as incomplete, with the reportable-fringe-benefit row unknown.
- Keep pension eligibility, illness-separated category, combined income and each individual offset unresolved.
- Identify the spouse eligibility and tax-position evidence needed for any transfer and hold the dependent seniors Medicare threshold open.
- Request exempt pension income for the donor's transfer calculation and require recalculation of the recipient's rebate/shade-out threshold before applying the reduction to the combined offset.
- Distinguish the resident, foreign-resident and qualifying-pension exception branches; request donor residency and payment evidence and leave transfer amounts conditional on applicable-period authority.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not substitute taxable income for rebate income or replace an unknown addition with zero.
- Do not award eligibility from age, retirement or living separately; do not transfer an assumed refund.
- Do not add an unused spouse amount directly to an already reduced recipient offset.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
