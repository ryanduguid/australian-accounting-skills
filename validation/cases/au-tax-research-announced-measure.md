# Client email mixing a Budget measure with routine questions

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented client emails 3 questions: whether a deduction announced in a Budget speech can be claimed this year, whether a receipt can be photographed instead of kept on paper, and whether a gift of shares to a sibling has tax consequences.

## Task

Use `au-tax-research` to triage the email and prepare a research file note for the announced measure.

## Synthetic inputs

The email quotes a media release saying the deduction 'will apply from 1 July this year'. The client is an individual employee. No bill name, bill number or legislation reference is supplied. The firm's research policy defines high, medium and low risk.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The measure's current parliamentary status, its start date in any enacted law and the share gift's facts remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- List the 3 questions separately with the sentence raising each, a risk rating under the firm's policy and where each routes; the share gift goes to its own research or to `au-capital-gains`.
- Record the measure's status from the Parliament's bills pages or the Federal Register, distinguishing announced, introduced, passed, assented and commenced; a media release is not law.
- Write the note on the law in force and set the announced change beside it with the ATO's guidance on proposed measures.
- Leave the choice of following the existing law or anticipating the change to the reviewer.
- Mark each source's currency label and record open facts with owner and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not tell the client the deduction can be claimed, merge the 3 questions into one reply or treat the media release's start date as enacted.
- Do not send the reply, lodge, sign or contact third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual income year and record its status on the date read. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the position and communicates it. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
