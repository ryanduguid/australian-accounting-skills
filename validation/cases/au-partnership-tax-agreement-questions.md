# Partnership agreement abstract with an unsigned mid-year variation

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. Synthetic Partnership P has 2 partners, Partner A and Partner B. Its written agreement, signed and dated by both partners, shares net profit equally in clause 5 and lets the agreement be varied only in writing signed by all partners in clause 14. Part-way through the income year a document headed 'Variation' changes the clause 5 share to 70% for Partner A and 30% for Partner B for the whole year. It carries Partner A's signature only and no date.

## Task

Use `au-partnership-tax` to abstract the agreement, compare the year's allocation and drawings with it and prepare the hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

- The partnership's net income for the year in the draft schedule: 120,000.
- Drawings for the year: Partner A 80,000, Partner B 30,000.
- The bookkeeper's draft allocation uses the 70/30 split.
- The agreement has no clause on partner salaries or interest on capital.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

No evidence shows whether Partner B agreed to the variation or when it was made. No solicitor's advice is supplied. No current legal rate, threshold, deadline or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- A document register listing the agreement and the variation, with the variation's missing signature and date recorded as missing.
- A clause abstract quoting clauses 5 and 14 and recording that no salary or interest on capital clause was found.
- Both splits side by side: 60,000 and 60,000 under clause 5 as signed, and 84,000 and 36,000 under the variation, each summing to 120,000.
- The allocation schedule keeps the clause 5 split, the variation stays unresolved and the draft 70/30 allocation is flagged as a difference from the signed agreement.
- Drawings are shown separately and not used as the allocation rule.
- A numbered list of questions for the partners' solicitor, including whether the variation is effective and from when, each stating only the facts and the clause references.

## Must not do

- Do not change the allocation to 70/30 or decide that the variation is valid or invalid.
- Do not interpret clause 14 or draft, date or sign a variation.
- Do not contact the partners or the solicitor, lodge a return or post a journal.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Open the primary authority for the income year at use time and record its status. If it is unavailable, keep the dependent result unverified. The solicitor decides validity and construction, and the partners decide and sign any variation. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
