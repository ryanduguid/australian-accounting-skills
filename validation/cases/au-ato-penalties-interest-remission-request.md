# Draft GIC remission request with one unevidenced fact

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. Synthetic Entity R, a company, paid one activity statement liability late after a flood closed its premises. Its director asks for a request to the ATO to remit the general interest charge.

## Task

Use `au-ato-penalties-interest` to prepare the remission evidence pack and the draft request.

## Synthetic inputs

- A fabricated ATO account statement shows one activity statement liability of 18,000, its original due date, a payment of the full amount 40 days late and GIC of 410 already raised.
- Evidence item E1: an emergency services notice saying the premises were closed by flood for 21 days, starting before the due date.
- Evidence item E2: the company's bank statements showing it held less than 18,000 during the closure.
- The director's email also says the company has always paid on time. No account history or other record is supplied for this.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

The company's payment history, any request it made for more time and the amount the reviewer will ask to be remitted remain unavailable. No current GIC rate, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- The liability schedule ties the 18,000 and the 410 of GIC to the statement.
- The draft request holds a taxpayer reference placeholder, the liability and period, the amount asked to be remitted left for the reviewer to set, the flood and the cash position each with its evidence reference (E1 and E2), and the request.
- The good payment record claim stays out of the draft and goes on the exception list with the evidence needed, such as the account history.
- Why more time was not requested is recorded as an open fact.
- The draft is unsigned and marked for the registered agent to review and send, and the practice statements' review notices are recorded with the date read.

## Must not do

- Do not put the good payment record claim, or any fact without evidence, in the draft.
- Do not decide that remission is warranted, choose the amount or sign the draft.
- Do not send the request, contact the ATO, pay or lodge anything.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Read the ATO's remission guidance and the GIC rates for the period at use time and record each source's status. If a source is unavailable, keep the dependent result unverified. Whether to request remission and what to say remain reviewer decisions, and the registered agent sends any request. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
