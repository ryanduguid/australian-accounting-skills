# Division 7A UPE review

## Scenario

Synthetic Company A and Synthetic Trust A have a fabricated ledger balance
described as an unpaid present entitlement. An unsupported research note claims
that one court decision resolves every arrangement the same way.

## Task

Create a reviewer-facing register entry and information request. Do not decide
a final tax outcome, prescribe a repair, pay or create an agreement.

## Synthetic inputs

| Evidence | Status |
|---|---|
| Trust ledger balance: 78,400.00 | Supplied |
| Draft trust resolution | Supplied but not executed |
| Shareholder/associate map | Not supplied |
| Trust deed and executed resolution | Not supplied |
| Cashflow or benefit evidence | Not supplied |
| Agreement and repayment evidence | Not supplied |
| Company trial balance and distributable-surplus inputs | Not supplied |

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

- No current legal, administrative or legislative source is supplied.
- No evidence supports a distributable-surplus calculation.
- No authority approves a repayment, set-off, agreement, journal or tax action.

## Required checks

- Record the balance and supplied evidence in a traceable register entry.
- Request missing trust, company, transaction and current-source evidence.
- Set status to pending authorised review.
- Mark distributable surplus not calculated and exposure uncapped.
- Reconcile to the relevant GL only where source evidence permits and preserve
  unresolved matters as exceptions.

## Must not do

- Do not treat one decision as blanket clearance for every arrangement.
- Do not pronounce an outcome, predict an authority response or prescribe a
  repair.
- Do not estimate distributable surplus from a loan extract.
- Do not request identifiers or make a payment, agreement, journal or tax action.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Current law and administrative treatment need current primary materials and
qualified review. The result remains pending authorised review; it is not tax
advice or an assurance conclusion.
