# Foreign pension net receipts with missing UPP and arrears evidence

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented resident supplies a foreign pension statement and requests an Australian assessable amount.

## Task

Use `au-foreign-pensions` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The statement records 8000.00 foreign-currency units received net, 1800.00 foreign tax withheld and a 200.00 fee deducted from the gross payment. It says some payment relates to earlier years but supplies no split. Exchange-rate evidence, personal-contribution history, UPP determination and treaty analysis are unavailable.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Reconcile gross payment to 10000.00 foreign-currency units and retain withholding and fees separately.
- Leave AUD conversion, UPP and the final Australian assessable amount unresolved.
- Ask for the payment character, treaty/residency, contribution and accrual-year evidence needed; distinguish periodic pension from a foreign-super lump sum.
- Preserve the foreign tax for separate eligibility and offset-limit review rather than claiming it as an automatic credit.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not treat the net receipt as gross income, deduct total UPP as the annual amount or use an invented exchange rate.
- Do not move arrears to earlier-year returns merely because they accrued then.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
