# Canberra home rented out part-way through a quarter beside a commercial unit

## Scenario

All records below are fabricated from scratch. An invented individual moved out of her Canberra home and rented it out, with the lease starting part-way through a quarter. She also owns a commercial unit in Canberra leased to a business. She asks the firm what land tax applies to each.

## Task

Use `au-land-tax` to prepare the ACT holdings review and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The residential lease, the commercial lease and the rates notices for both properties are supplied. No land tax assessment notice has been received, and her notice delivery preference is email.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The fixed charge, rates and thresholds, the notification period, any notice she gave the ACT Revenue Office and her foreign status remain missing. No legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Route the land to the ACT Revenue Office and the Land Tax Act 2004, and read the charges, rates and notification period from the office.
- Record each property's status on the first day of each quarter from the leases and occupancy evidence, and record that the office assesses whole quarters with no pro-rating within one.
- Record that the office says land tax does not apply to commercial property, and keep the commercial unit out of the land tax schedule.
- Record that the owner must tell the office when a property becomes liable, and request evidence of any notice she gave.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not pro-rate land tax within a quarter, apply the foreign ownership surcharge without foreign status evidence or apply a rate from memory.
- Do not notify, pay or contact the ACT Revenue Office.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the ACT Revenue Office's current land tax guidance and the Land Tax Act 2004 for the actual quarters. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
