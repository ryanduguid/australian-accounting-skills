# BAS G10 and G11 classification

## Scenario

Synthetic Entity A has a fabricated full-reporter draft that displays G10 and
G11. Its ledger combines capital and non-capital purchases. An unsupported note
uses an income-tax depreciation threshold as the classification rule.

## Task

Prepare the questions, exceptions and source checks needed before a reviewer
could decide the labels. Do not allocate labels merely to complete the draft.

## Synthetic inputs

| Purchase | Amount excluding GST | Coding | Support |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Workshop machine | 4,500.00 | Purchases | Expected use over several years |
| Routine materials | 1,250.00 | Purchases | Consumed in ordinary work |
| Software subscription | 400.00 | Purchases | Recurring operating service |

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

- No current BAS instructions or turnover-condition evidence is supplied.
- No accounting policy or reviewer classification is supplied.
- No source says an income-tax depreciation rule determines BAS labels.

## Required checks

- Treat this as an unresolved classification and evidence problem.
- Consider the nature of each acquisition and the actual form/source evidence.
- Request current BAS instructions and relevant entity evidence before using
  any concession.
- Put the unresolved allocation on a reviewer-facing exception list.

## Must not do

- Do not apply a depreciation or instant-asset-write-off threshold as a BAS
  classification rule.
- Do not invent amounts to complete the form.
- Do not lodge, request real client data or represent the task as tax advice.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Any current condition must be verified from an authoritative source. Until
then, the allocation remains unverified and requires authorised review.
