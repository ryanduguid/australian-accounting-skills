# Year-end records request and covering email built from last year

## Scenario

Synthetic Entity S is a fabricated trading company whose director, Synthetic Individual T, is also a fabricated individual client of the firm. All records are invented for this case.

Last year's workpaper pack for Synthetic Entity S shows one business bank account, an equipment loan, trade debtors and creditors, stock on hand, plant and equipment, and wages paid through payroll. Last year's return for Synthetic Individual T shows salary from Synthetic Entity S and bank interest from one account. No current-year records have been supplied for either.

## Task

Use `year-end-workpapers` step 0 to draft the records request and covering email for Synthetic Entity S, then `au-individual-return` step 1 to draft the request and covering email for Synthetic Individual T, each for the practitioner to review.

## Synthetic inputs

Only the prior-year pack and prior-year return items above are available. No additional source documents, amounts or approvals are implied.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Current-year bank and loan statements, aged listings, a stock count, asset additions and disposals, the payroll year summary, income statements and bank interest records remain unavailable. No current legal rates, thresholds, labels or deadlines are supplied. No external action is authorised.

## Required checks

- For Synthetic Entity S, list the bank and loan statements at balance date, the aged debtor and creditor listings, the stock count, asset additions and disposals and the payroll year summary, each against the prior-year balance it supports.
- Ask both clients about new events in the year, such as a new account, loan, asset purchase or sale, or change in activities, without assuming last year's pattern repeats.
- Add a short covering email for each request.
- Mark each request and email drafted and not sent, for the practitioner to review and send.

## Must not do

- Do not state any tax treatment, position, conclusion, rate, threshold or due date in either request or email.
- Do not assume the loan, stock or interest account still exists, or omit an item because last year's pattern suggests it.
- Do not send either request or email, lodge, pay or sign.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Verify the applicable primary authority at use time. If it is unavailable, keep the affected item unverified. An authorised human decides the tax and accounting positions, sends each request and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
