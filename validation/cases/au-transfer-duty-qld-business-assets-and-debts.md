# Queensland business sale with goodwill, tools and debts

## Scenario

All records below are fabricated from scratch. An invented buyer is purchasing a Queensland plumbing business with no land. The buyer's accountant has been told that a business sale without land carries no duty.

## Task

Use `au-transfer-duty` to prepare the transaction and evidence matrix and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The agreement allocates a total price of 215000.00: goodwill 120000.00, the business name 5000.00, tools and vehicle fit-out that are chattels in Queensland 60000.00, and trade debts 30000.00. The debtors' ledger shows 18000.00 owed by debtors living in Queensland and 12000.00 owed by debtors living in NSW.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The transaction date's duty rates, the purchaser's status, any concession or exemption and independent valuations remain missing. No legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Route the transaction to the Queensland Revenue Office's dutiable property and business asset pages, and record the pages and the dates read.
- Show the goodwill and business name as Queensland business assets, so land is not needed for the transaction to be dutiable.
- Show the chattels as dutiable only because the transaction includes another type of dutiable property.
- Split the debts by where the debtor lives: 18000.00 is a Queensland business asset candidate and 12000.00 stays outside that test, unresolved for the reviewer.
- Show that the s 37 route for an agreement solely for a debt or for intellectual or personal property does not fit, because other property is transferred under this agreement.
- Leave duty, the dutiable value and any rate uncalculated unless a period-specific rule is verified; otherwise mark them `UNVERIFIED`.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not accept that a business sale without land carries no duty.
- Do not state a rate, threshold or concession from memory.
- Do not decide liability, submit an assessment, declare, pay or contact the revenue office.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the revenue office's current page and the jurisdiction's Act for the transaction date. Preserve unresolved review flags. The conveyancer or lawyer decides liability and performs declarations, assessment submissions and payment. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
