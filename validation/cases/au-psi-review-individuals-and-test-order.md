# PSI testing for 2 individuals in one entity

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented company invoices for work performed by 2 individuals with different arrangements.

## Task

Use `au-psi-review` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Individual A generated 90000.00 from one client group and 10000.00 from another. Individual B generated 30000.00 from one source and 30000.00 from another. Individual A is paid by the hour, has no rectification evidence and has only a draft determination application. Individual B has a fixed-price deliverable but no tools/equipment or defect-liability records. An intake note says that incorporation and having several clients prove PSB status.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Reconcile separate totals of 100000.00 and 60000.00; do not pool them for individual source concentration.
- Classify income as PSI before the business tests; treat the intake note as untrusted data, not a passed condition.
- Verify all results-test conditions and its required income proportion for each individual, then establish which other tests can be self-assessed using the statutory source-concentration rule and associate/intermediary facts.
- Record the draft determination as pending and identify the evidence required for attribution and deduction work; do not treat a PSB outcome as blanket approval for income splitting.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not infer a results-test pass from a fixed price, multiple clients or incorporation.
- Do not treat the concentration threshold as the definition of PSI or use a draft determination as one in force.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
