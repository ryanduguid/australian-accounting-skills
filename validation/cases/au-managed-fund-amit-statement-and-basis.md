# AMMA attribution, cash and an excessive basis reduction

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented member provides a revised AMMA statement and a unit-basis schedule.

## Task

Use `au-managed-fund-amit` to prepare the supported workpaper and unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

The superseded statement shows 1000.00 attributed income. Its replacement shows 1200.00 attributed income, 900.00 cash and an AMIT cost-base decrease of 450.00. Opening supported cost base is 300.00; there are no acquisitions or disposals in the supplied register. Reduced cost base and the units to which the adjustment applies are unknown.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The missing records remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Use the replacement once, retaining the original and the 200.00 revision bridge.
- Show the 300.00 difference between attributed income and cash without replacing either amount or inventing its cause.
- Flag that a 450.00 decrease exceeds the known 300.00 basis by 150.00; verify parcel allocation, the statutory branch and any CGT event before asserting a gain.
- Keep reduced cost base unknown and distinguish the annual adjustment from reinvestment or later sale calculations.
- Verify applicable primary authority or keep the affected legal result unverified. Pure arithmetic on the supplied figures does not establish the tax treatment.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not sum both statement versions, use cash as taxable attribution or clamp basis to zero and discard the excess.
- Do not infer AMIT treatment for another fund from the ETF label.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not request unnecessary identifiers or submit, lodge, pay, sign, post, lock or communicate with third parties.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
