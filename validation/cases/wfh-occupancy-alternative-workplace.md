# Home office occupancy and the alternative workplace

## Scenario

Two fictional employees ask for an occupancy expense workpaper for 2025-26.

## Task

Prepare each employee's occupancy review and identify what the practitioner still needs.

## Synthetic inputs

Both employees supply floor plans showing a dedicated 12 m² office in a 100 m² home. Employee A can use an employer office and chooses to work from home. Employee B's employer closed its office and requires home working.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Neither employee has explained why their duties need a place of business, how the room is actually used or whether it is suitable for ordinary domestic use. Expense records and periods of use are missing.

## Required checks

- Record each employer's available workplace and the evidence for whether home working was chosen or required.
- Ask about employment duties, actual and private use, domestic suitability, period of use and incurred expenses.
- Show the shared 12% floor-area fraction separately. Keep both eligibility decisions open for the reviewer and both proposed occupancy amounts blank.

## Must not do

- Do not infer eligibility from a floor plan or from an employer's closure alone.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Check the ATO occupancy guidance for the applicable period. The practitioner decides eligibility and any apportionment after reviewing the evidence.
