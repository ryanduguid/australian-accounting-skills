# Short-paid quarter and paydays remitted but not shown as received

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented company employer short-paid super for one employee in the quarter ending 31 March 2026, then paid 2 later paydays through a clearing house. The director asks for 'the SGC figure' so the company can pay it this week.

## Task

Use `au-super-guarantee-charge` to prepare the shortfall register and the hand-off for the registered agent.

## Synthetic inputs

Employee E1, quarter ending 31 March 2026: ordinary time earnings 10000.00, salary or wages 11000.00, contributions received by the fund before the quarter's due date 600.00 and a further 300.00 received by the fund in May 2026. No late payment offset election has been made. Payday P1 in August 2026 and payday P2 in September 2026: qualifying earnings 4000.00 each, with a clearing house remittance recorded 2 business days after each payday. No fund receipt or allocation confirmation exists for either payday. The company has 2 directors.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The super guarantee rate sources, general interest charge rates, fund receipt dates for P1 and P2, choice of fund records and the employer's assessment history are missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Keep the March 2026 quarter in an old-Act register and P1 and P2 in a Payday Super register, recording the compilation used for each.
- Carry both ordinary time earnings and salary or wages for the quarter; leave the required contribution and every component blank and `UNVERIFIED` until each rate and formula is read from a primary source.
- Record the 300.00 as a late contribution that could support a late payment offset only if the employer elects it, and leave the election to the employer on the registered agent's advice.
- Mark the timing of P1 and P2 `UNKNOWN`, because a clearing house remittance is not fund receipt, and request fund receipt and allocation evidence.
- Note that a voluntary disclosure statement can include payment days, that the s 36(3) deeming affects notional earnings only and that lodging one is the employer's decision.
- Flag the director penalty question for the registered agent and a lawyer without a conclusion.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not state a charge payable, a rate, a due date or an interest figure, and do not compute any component from an unverified input.
- Do not treat a remittance date as receipt or deem a receipt day for the final shortfall.
- Do not invent facts, authority or approval; do not lodge, pay, sign, elect or contact the ATO, the fund, the directors' lawyer or the employee.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use applicable primary authority for the actual work period. Preserve unresolved review flags. An authorised human decides the tax position and performs consequential actions. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
