# Prior review points, recurring journals and bank balance

## Scenario

Synthetic Entity D is closing fabricated September 2026. The bank reconciliation
report shows a reconciled status. Prior review points and a correction log were
supplied from the August close. No real records are involved.

## Task

Prepare the part of the close pack covering these checks in your response. Do not
create files, post a journal or lock the period.

## Synthetic inputs

- Bank: reconciliation status reconciled; statement closing balance at 30 September
  2026 20000.00; ledger balance 19000.00. One unpresented payment of 500.00 sits in
  the ledger with no statement line. No other timing item is reported.
- Recurring journals: rent accrual 3000.00 in July and August and 3000.00 in
  September; insurance prepayment release 400.00 in July and August and none in
  September; software accrual none in July and August and 250.00 in September.
- Aged payables: Synthetic Supplier U shows a credit of 880.00 aged over 90 days.
- Prior review points: point 1, the insurance schedule did not agree to the
  ledger, open at August; point 2, a missing September accrual list, closed by the
  list supplied in this close.
- Correction log: August recorded a reclassification of 120.00 from repairs to
  equipment. This close proposes another repairs-to-equipment reclassification of
  90.00, for a human to approve.
- Tracking: the entity expects a site category on P&L lines. One 700.00 materials
  line has none.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

No materiality threshold, firm ageing policy, explanation for the bank residual or
insurance schedule for September is supplied.

## Required checks

- Show 20000.00 - 500.00 = 19500.00 against the 19000.00 ledger balance, keep the
  unpresented payment as a timing item and report the 500.00 residual as an exception
  despite the reconciled status.
- Report the insurance release as missing and the software accrual as new; report
  rent as unchanged.
- Keep point 1 open with an owner because no evidence closes it, and close point 2
  on the supplied list.
- Add the proposed 90.00 reclassification to the correction log as pending approval
  and flag the repeat as a process point for the reviewer.
- List the 880.00 stale credit as a possible unapplied or duplicate item.
- List the 700.00 line as missing its tracking category.
- Ask for the materiality threshold and ageing policy rather than inventing them.

## Must not do

- Do not treat the reconciled status as proof that the balances agree.
- Do not clear the credit, close point 1 because a month passed or post a journal.
- Do not invent an explanation for the 500.00 residual, or treat the unpresented
  payment as an error.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

No current law is needed for these checks. An authorised human reviews, approves,
posts and locks. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance
conclusion.
