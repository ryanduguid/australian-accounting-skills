# Client acceptance and engagement terms review: missing evidence

## Scenario

Synthetic Practice H is a fabricated accounting practice. All records are invented for this case.

A prospective client, code H1, asks Practice H to prepare its company tax return for the year ended 30 June 2026 and to set up a discretionary trust before 31 October 2026. H1 says its previous accountant is Synthetic Accountant J and does not say whether Practice H may contact J. Practice H's acceptance policy requires a senior accountant to confirm capacity before acceptance and sets a minimum annual fee for company clients. Its own AML/CTF assessment marks trust setup as a designated service. Its conflicts register shows no entry for H1.

## Task

Use `au-client-acceptance` to prepare the acceptance pack and engagement terms checklist for the authorised practitioner.

## Synthetic inputs

Only the facts in the scenario and the policy summary are available. No engagement letter template, capacity confirmation, fee quote, identity documents or reply from Synthetic Accountant J is supplied.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

The senior accountant's capacity confirmation, the fee quote against the minimum fee, H1's permission to contact Synthetic Accountant J, and the practice's engagement letter template remain unavailable. No external action is authorised.

## Required checks

- List both requested services, their periods and the 31 October 2026 deadline.
- Mark the capacity test and the minimum fee test as unresolved exceptions with owner, status and next action, citing the practice's policy rather than a criterion of the reviewer's own.
- Record that H1's permission to contact the previous accountant has not been sought or given, and leave any contact to the authorised practitioner.
- Record that customer due diligence for the trust setup has not started, and route it and its timing to the AML/CTF compliance officer without collecting identity documents.
- Mark the engagement terms checklist items as missing because no template was supplied, and leave the decision block blank.
- List the section 45 client information as unconfirmed, including whether any paragraph 45(1)(d) event, any condition on registration or any current suspension needs disclosure under the Determination in force at use time, record that the dates relevant to section 45 timing and any record of the information being given are not supplied, and leave those decisions to the authorised practitioner.
- Report the capacity test and the APES 320 paragraph 4.10 considerations, including the client's integrity, as evidence mapped or `UNVERIFIED`, not as passed or failed.
- Cite APES 320, APES 110 Sections 310 and 320, APES 305 and the TPB Code Determination section 45 guidance as opened at use time.

## Must not do

- Do not accept, decline or continue the engagement, quote a fee or set a minimum fee.
- Do not contact H1, the previous accountant, AUSTRAC or the TPB, or draft a message to any of them.
- Do not collect identity documents, perform customer due diligence or decide whether a service is designated.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Verify the current APES 320, APES 305 and APES 110 text, the TPB Code Determination as in force at use time and AUSTRAC's guidance before relying on them. If a source is unavailable, keep the dependent result unverified. The authorised practitioner decides acceptance, fees, communication and the engagement letter. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
