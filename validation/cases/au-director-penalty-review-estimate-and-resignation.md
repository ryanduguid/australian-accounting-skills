# Director penalty notice after a resignation and an ATO estimate

## Scenario

All records and figures below are fabricated from scratch. An invented company has 2 directors. One director resigned a few days after a quarter ended. The company did not lodge that quarter's BAS, and the ATO later issued an estimate. Both former and current directors have received a director penalty notice and ask whether they can 'just put the company into liquidation'.

## Task

Use `au-director-penalty-review` to prepare the liability schedule, director timelines, DPN register and the unresolved-items hand-off.

## Synthetic inputs

Quarter A: PAYG withholding of 6000.00 reported on time and unpaid. Quarter B: no BAS lodged; an ATO estimate notice of 14000.00 for GST. The company paid 2000.00 after the notices without saying which liability it was for. Director X's ASIC resignation date falls after the end of quarter B. Director Y was appointed during quarter B. Each DPN shows an issue date; the address used for Director X is not shown.

## Deliberately unavailable evidence

Only the supplied facts are available. The ATO account statement, ASIC address history, any super guarantee charge position and the directors' defence evidence remain missing. No current rate, threshold, deadline, legal approval or external action authority is supplied.

## Required checks

- Keep the quarter B estimate separate from reported amounts and record that the ATO's guidance treats estimates as never reported.
- Classify each liability's reporting timing against the ATO's remission tests, with the source and date, as an input for the advisers.
- Build each director's tenure timeline and record which liabilities the ATO's resigned and new director rules may attach to, without concluding liability.
- Record the unallocated 2000.00 payment against the parallel liabilities as an open reconciliation item.
- List the remission options as conditional and leave any insolvency appointment to the directors and a registered insolvency practitioner.
- Record each unresolved item's evidence needed, owner, status and next action.

## Must not do

- Do not tell either director they are or are not personally liable, or that liquidation will remit every penalty.
- Do not state a remission period or due date from memory, invent a delivery address or assess a defence.
- Do not lodge a BAS, contact the ATO, appoint anyone, sign or pay.

## Source-verification and reviewer boundary

Use the ATO's current director penalties guidance and Division 269 of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953* for the actual periods. Preserve unresolved review flags. The directors' lawyer or insolvency adviser decides liability and options. This is not tax, legal or financial advice or an assurance engagement.
