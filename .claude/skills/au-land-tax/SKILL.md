# State land tax assessment review

## Inputs

State or territory, assessment year and notice, ownership at the jurisdiction's taxing date, land values, holdings in relevant capacities, trust/company documents, use and occupancy evidence, foreign-owner facts, prior assessments and, for Victorian rezoned land, windfall gains tax notices and deferral records.

## Workflow

1. Create a jurisdiction-specific holdings register. Confirm that the named jurisdiction imposes the relevant tax and identify its taxing date before doing calculations.

   Route the work to the revenue office and Act in the jurisdiction's row below. Record who owned each parcel at the taxing time the row describes, from title, contract and settlement evidence, and obtain the assessment or notice from where the row says the owner finds it. The table states no rate, threshold, taxing date, due date or fee: read each from the revenue office's current page for the assessment year.

   **Human review pending:** an agent read the revenue office pages listed under Primary-source starting point on 4 November 2026 and checked each Act link against its legislation register or, for NSW, against Revenue NSW's own link to it (not a human review).

   | Jurisdiction | Revenue office | Act | Owner at the taxing time | Assessment or notice | Surcharge or separate tax named |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | New South Wales | Revenue NSW | [Land Tax Act 1956](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-1956-027) and [Land Tax Management Act 1956](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-1956-026) | Revenue NSW assesses all non-exempt land owned at midnight on the taxing date and charges the tax for the full year that follows it | An assessment notice for land tax and surcharge land tax, sent by post or, to an owner who opted in, by email; a copy can be downloaded in Land Tax Online | Surcharge land tax, for foreign owners of residential land |
   | Victoria | State Revenue Office Victoria | [Land Tax Act 2005](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/land-tax-act-2005) | The owner at midnight on the taxing date in the previous year pays, and an owner who sells during the year remains responsible for the full year's land tax | A land tax assessment, which the owner can view and download in My Land Tax; the owner can also link the State Revenue Office to myGov | The absentee owner surcharge, which appears in the land tax assessment, and vacant residential land tax, a separate tax with its own assessment. Windfall gains tax is step 5 |
   | Queensland | Queensland Revenue Office | [Land Tax Act 2010](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-2010-015) | Freehold land owned at midnight on the taxing date, for the next financial year; a sale after it does not divide the liability between buyer and seller | An assessment notice, by post or by an email saying it is available in QRO Online | The absentee surcharge and the foreign surcharge, which appear on the assessment notice |
   | Western Australia | RevenueWA (Department of Treasury and Finance) | [Land Tax Assessment Act 2002](https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/statutes.nsf/law_a6341.html) and [Land Tax Act 2002](https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/statutes.nsf/law_a6340.html) | Ownership at midnight on the taxing date of the previous assessment year; an owner who sells during the year is still liable on that ownership | A Notice of Assessment, by email to an owner registered for email notices; the land tax online services portal shows the owner's land holdings and account | None named on the page read. Metropolitan region improvement tax may be payable with land tax on land in the Perth metropolitan region |
   | South Australia | RevenueSA | [Land Tax Act 1936](https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/lz?path=%2FC%2FA%2FLAND%20TAX%20ACT%201936) | Ownership at midnight on the taxing date; a vendor who sells after it remains liable for the full land tax for the upcoming financial year | A Land Tax Assessment (formerly a Notice of Land Tax Assessment), by email to an owner who signed up for email billing; separate assessments can issue for different ownership structures, land held on trust and corporate groups | None named on the page read |
   | Tasmania | State Revenue Office Tasmania | [Land Tax Act 2000](https://www.legislation.tas.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-2000-074) and [Land Tax Rating Act 2000](https://www.legislation.tas.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-2000-073) | The owner of land classified as General Land at the taxing date each year pays; land classified as Principal Residence Land is not subject to land tax | A land tax notice, by email to an owner who asked for email delivery | The foreign investor land tax surcharge, on General Land |
   | Australian Capital Territory | ACT Revenue Office | [Land Tax Act 2004](https://www.legislation.act.gov.au/a/2004-4/) | Assessed quarterly on the property's status on the first day of each quarter, for whole quarters with no pro-rating within one. Land tax applies to residential property that is not the owner's principal place of residence, such as rented or vacant property and property owned by a corporation or held as trustee, and does not apply to commercial property. An owner must tell the office when a property is or becomes liable | A land tax assessment notice, by email or post as the owner's notice delivery preferences for property charges set | The foreign ownership surcharge |
   | Northern Territory | Territory Revenue Office | None listed: the office's list of the main revenue Acts it administers names no land tax Act | Not stated on the page read | Not stated on the page read | None named |

   The Territory Revenue Office page does not say in terms that the Territory levies no land tax. For Territory land, record that no land tax authority was found, mark any land tax result `UNVERIFIED` and prepare an enquiry item for the responsible practitioner to obtain the office's confirmation before treating the land as untaxed. Do not contact the office: an authorised human handles the enquiry. The legislation register links are not indexed in `sources.json` yet. Confirm each row against the current page and the Act for the assessment year before relying on it. A row decides no liability, exemption or surcharge.

2. Compare each assessment parcel, owner/capacity, value and exemption claim with source records. Keep separately owned, jointly owned and trust-held land distinguishable.

3. Verify aggregation, thresholds, concessions, exemptions and foreign-owner surcharge rules with that jurisdiction's revenue office and legislation for the assessment year.

4. Reconcile the proposed assessment to the notice, explain differences and identify the supported review deadline. Use NSW material only for NSW; obtain the corresponding authority before handling another jurisdiction. Check the tax on the verified taxable value with `land-tax`, and any foreign or absentee owner surcharge with `foreign-purchaser-surcharge`, in [Tax calculators](#tax-calculators).

5. **Victorian windfall gains tax.** For Victorian land affected by a government rezoning, keep windfall gains tax separate from land tax. The State Revenue Office describes it as charged when the land is rezoned, not when it is sold or developed, payable by the owner at the time of rezoning, on the uplift in capital improved value that the Valuer-General Victoria determines. Record the rezoning event and date, the owner at that time, the before and after values on the notice, each exemption or waiver claimed with its evidence, and any deferral: the election, the amount deferred, the interest accruing and the events the State Revenue Office lists as ending deferral. Where deferred land is subdivided, reconcile the apportionment to each child lot. Read the threshold, rates and payment deadlines from the State Revenue Office at use time. Route a proposed sale or transfer to `au-transfer-duty` so the deferral consequence is checked with the duty work. Check the tax on the notice's values with `vic-property-taxes` in [Tax calculators](#tax-calculators).

## Site calculators

The site's [land tax calculator](https://duguid.com.au/tools/business-calculators/land-tax/) (published and last checked 16 November 2026, read 23 November 2026) applies one state or territory's general or trust scale, with any foreign or absentee owner surcharge, to a total taxable land value for a full year, at the rates it read on 16 November 2026. It does not decide which land is taxable, which rates apply, whether an owner is a foreign person or absentee, or the taxable or surcharge value, and it leaves out the exemptions, concessions and other cases the page lists by jurisdiction; it has no foreign or absentee rates for Western Australia or South Australia and no trust rates for Western Australia. The site's [foreign purchaser surcharge calculator](https://duguid.com.au/tools/business-calculators/foreign-surcharge/) (published 18 November 2026, last checked 19 November 2026) applies each jurisdiction's test of a foreign or absentee owner. It does not decide exemptions, refunds or ex gratia relief, does not cover joint land tax assessments, and does not trace interests through other companies and trusts. Each runs in the browser and sends no inputs to a server. Before relying on one, confirm it supports the reporting month or land tax year in question; use it as a cross-check of step 4's reconciliation only, never as a fallback when a local tool refuses. Enter only values and the owner category, never the owner's name, a property address or a title reference. Record what it consumed: the page, the date run, the inputs and the result. Keep the page's own labels as its own, beside those inputs. Where a page and the assessment or workpaper differ, record the difference for the reviewer and adopt neither.

Run it by its full path, the extracted `australian-tax-calculators` or `<tool-dir>/bin/australian-tax-calculators` (`.exe` on Windows), never one found on `PATH`, as other builds also report 1.4.0; check that `--version` prints exactly `australian-tax-calculators 1.4.0`, and pass `--refusal-json` after the command so a refusal (exit 2) prints its code. Use this local executable; a remote calculator is used only where the user has explicitly permitted that call for this question, on fabricated or firm-approved inputs, and never as a fallback when a local tool refuses. Each command reads one JSON object on stdin, without the `kind` field that only `periods` takes, with amounts as decimal strings and a `scope_confirmed` flag: set it only once each condition in the scope statement the command returns is evidenced, and where one is not, follow the written method for that item. Send only amounts, dates and the established facts the command takes, never a client's name, address, TFN, ABN or other identifier. Record what it consumed: the release, the engine version, the exact input, the output with the scope statement, sources and source-check date it returns, and the run date. Record a refusal and its code as an open item, and do not change the facts to get past it. Keep the calculator's own labels as its own, reported beside the facts it was given, never as the reviewer's decision on a position. A calculator's figure checks the written method, which is always completed: where they differ, record the difference for the reviewer and adopt neither. Without the executable, follow the written method.

`land-tax` works out one owner's land tax for a full land tax year: the 2026 land tax year in New South Wales and Victoria, and 2026-27 in Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory; the Northern Territory has no land tax. The operator establishes the jurisdiction's total taxable value of the owner's land after exemptions and concessions, whether trust rates apply, whether the owner pays a foreign or absentee surcharge and on what value, and in Western Australia whether the land is all in the Perth metropolitan region. It does not decide which land is taxable, exemptions, concessions or who is foreign or absentee, and it leaves out joint ownership, grouping, single holding assessments, part-year and supplementary assessments and the other cases its scope statement lists, so steps 1 to 3 stay as written. On fabricated facts, a Queensland individual with a taxable value of 950,000 and no trust rates or surcharge, it gave land tax of 4,000.00 for 2026-27 (run on 26 November 2026 with the verified 1.4.0 Windows executable).

`foreign-purchaser-surcharge` works out a foreign or absentee owner land tax surcharge for a full land tax year (2026 in New South Wales and Victoria, 2026-27 in Queensland, Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory) at the rates in force when read on 24 November 2026; Western Australia, South Australia and the Northern Territory charge none. It also takes the dutiable value of a purchase and reports the foreign purchaser duty surcharge beside the land tax surcharge; only the land tax surcharge belongs in this workpaper. The operator establishes whether the owner is a foreign person or absentee under the jurisdiction's land tax test, what is residential land and the values. It refuses a Victorian trust that is a foreign owner and does not decide exemptions, refunds or the Commissioner's discretions. On fabricated facts, a foreign individual owning New South Wales residential land valued at 900,000, it gave a land tax surcharge of 45,000.00 (run on 26 November 2026 with the verified 1.4.0 Windows executable).

`vic-property-taxes` works out one Victorian tax on land for the 2025 or 2026 tax year on the values the operator states: windfall gains tax on one event, vacant residential land tax on one parcel, or the commercial and industrial property tax. For windfall gains tax it takes the Valuer-General's values before and after for every title the one event rezoned, owned by one assessment unit whose grouping is already settled. It does not decide exemptions, waivers, deferral or interest, or apportion the tax to titles, so step 5's record of them stands. On fabricated facts, one title rezoned in 2026 with capital improved values of 1,100,000 before and 1,600,000 after, it gave windfall gains tax of 250,000.00 (run on 26 November 2026 with the verified 1.4.0 Windows executable).

## Primary-source starting point

One revenue office per state and territory. Step 1 records what each page said when an agent read it.

- New South Wales: [Revenue NSW land tax assessment notices (NSW only)](https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au/taxes-duties-levies-royalties/land-tax/your-assessment-notice/understand-your-assessment-notice)
- Victoria: [State Revenue Office Victoria: Understanding land tax](https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/owning-property/land-tax/understanding-land-tax), [Understanding vacant residential land tax](https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/owning-property/vacant-residential-land-tax/understanding-vacant-residential-land-tax), [State Revenue Office Victoria: Understanding windfall gains tax (Victoria only)](https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/owning-property/windfall-gains-tax/understanding-windfall-gains-tax) and [State Revenue Office Victoria: Defer a windfall gains tax liability (Victoria only)](https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/owning-property/windfall-gains-tax/defer-or-pay-windfall-gains-tax/defer-windfall-gains-tax-liability)
- Queensland: [Queensland Revenue Office: Land tax assessment notices explained](https://qro.qld.gov.au/land-tax/pay/assessment/)
- Western Australia: [RevenueWA: Land tax assessment](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/department-of-treasury-and-finance/land-tax-assessment)
- South Australia: [RevenueSA: Land Tax](https://revenuesa.sa.gov.au/landtax)
- Tasmania: [State Revenue Office Tasmania: Land Tax](https://www.sro.tas.gov.au/land-tax)
- Australian Capital Territory: [ACT Revenue Office: About land tax](https://www.revenue.act.gov.au/rates-and-property-charges/land-tax/about-land-tax) and [How land tax is calculated](https://www.revenue.act.gov.au/rates-and-property-charges/land-tax/how-land-tax-is-calculated)
- Northern Territory: [Territory Revenue Office: Legislation](https://treasury.nt.gov.au/dtf/territory-revenue-office/legislation)
