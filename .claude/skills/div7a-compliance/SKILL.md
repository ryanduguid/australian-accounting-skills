# Division 7A compliance

Build the Div 7A picture for a private company: what's been advanced, what's covered by a complying agreement, what must be repaid by when, and what's at risk of being a deemed dividend.

## Inputs needed

1. GL detail for shareholder/associate loan accounts, drawings, and intercompany accounts for the income year
2. Existing loan agreements (terms, dates, security)
3. Prior year Div 7A schedules and repayment history
4. The company's lodgement day for the year (actual or expected)
5. Trust structures in the group, if any (UPE exposure), including any loan agreement over a UPE and any prior assessment that included a deemed dividend because a UPE was treated as a loan
6. Year-end trial balance or draft financial statements for the company (net assets per the accounting records, provisions, paid-up share value) for the distributable surplus calculation

## Workflow

1. **Sweep for Div 7A events.** Loans, payments, and debt forgiveness to shareholders or their associates during the year, including transactions routed through drawings, 'temporary' advances, and assets used personally. List every candidate with account, counterparty, and amount.
2. **Classify each event.** Repaid in full before lodgement day / covered by complying agreement / new loan needing an agreement / potential deemed dividend. Note: a repayment is disregarded (s 109R of the ITAA 1936) where a reasonable person would conclude the borrower intended, when repaying, to obtain a similar or larger loan from the company, or where such a loan was obtained *before* the repayment and a reasonable person would conclude it was obtained to make it. Set-offs of dividends, and of salary or wages subject to withholding, are excepted (s 109R(3) of the ITAA 1936), so those count as repayments even with a re-borrow, and are the standard way clients meet minimum yearly repayments. Check the position against current ATO guidance (TD 2025/5) rather than asserting it from memory; where s 109R of the ITAA 1936 applies, treat the loan as still outstanding and flag the round-trip.
3. **Check agreements are complying.** Written, signed before lodgement day, term within the maximum (7 years unsecured; 25 years where properly secured over real property; verify current maximums at ato.gov.au), interest at or above the benchmark rate. Pull the current benchmark interest rate from ato.gov.au. It changes yearly; never use a remembered figure. If ato.gov.au is unreachable from this session, stop and ask the user for the current rate, record it as 'per [name], [date], unverified', and flag it on the workpaper. Never construct a citation from memory. <!-- rule: evidence-boundary -->
4. **Calculate minimum yearly repayments** for each complying loan using the ATO's formula with the current benchmark rate. Compare to actual repayments; shortfall = deemed dividend exposure for the year, capped by distributable surplus. MYR applies from the income year after the loan is made. For loans advanced this year, the action is a complying agreement (or repayment) before lodgement day, not an MYR.
5. **Distributable surplus.** Compute per the statutory formula. A deemed dividend can't exceed it; document the calculation. Without the company's trial balance or financial statements, do not compute it and do not approximate net assets from loan extracts or a prior year schedule. Mark it 'not calculated, exposure uncapped' on the register and flag it.
6. **Trust UPEs.** Identify unpaid present entitlements owed to corporate beneficiaries. The High Court held in *Bendel* [2026] HCA 18 that a UPE is not of itself a Div 7A loan; whether the arrangement went further than a UPE still turns on the deed and the facts, so flag that rather than assuming either way. Do not state the ATO's current administrative position from memory. Check and cite the decision impact statement, the current status of TD 2022/11 and TR 2010/3, and any legislative response, all as at the date of the work. If ato.gov.au is unreachable from this session, stop and ask the user, record it as 'per [name], [date], unverified', and flag it on the register. Never construct a citation from memory. Raise 2 further reviewer flags, citing the paragraphs as checked at the date of the work. First, list each UPE that has been made subject to complying loan terms, or otherwise satisfied, replaced or dealt with: the decision impact statement of 26 June 2026 says a UPE made subject to complying loan terms is a loan, a UPE dealt with in a way that gives rise to a loan within s 109D(3) is treated as one under the ITAA 1936, and *Bendel* does not reinstate a satisfied or converted entitlement as a mere UPE ([34], [38]). Second, list each prior year in which a deemed dividend was assessed on the basis that a UPE without more was a loan: that statement says the taxpayer may seek an amendment within the amendment period or object outside it, and that an extension of time for a late objection will be considered ([40]-[42]). Record the assessment, the amendment period evidence and the trust deed, trustee resolutions, accounting records and later dealings with the entitlement, which the statement says those decisions will consider ([41]), for the reviewer; do not decide whether to request an amendment or object. The position goes to the reviewer; this skill does not assert it.
7. **Output the register.** Per counterparty: opening balance, movements, agreement status, minimum repayment versus actual, exposure, action required before lodgement day. Use the firm-approved secure client-data location. If none is configured, ask before creating a path beside a checkout. Confirm the selected path is outside every version-control checkout, not merely ignored by one; do not change `.gitignore`, output locations or repository configuration without explicit approval.

## Local engine, when available

If the `aus-accounting` MCP tools are available in this session (the Claude Code plugin starts them), use them to check the arithmetic in steps 3 and 4. They read a bundled table and contact no service, so they never replace the step 3 source check:

- Complete step 3 first. Then call `get_div7a_benchmark_rate` with `response_detail` set to `full` for the income year and compare its rate with the one from ato.gov.au. `UNKNOWN` means the table does not cover the year.
- Only when the two rates agree, call `review_div7a_loan` once per amalgamated loan, passing only facts the records establish, and compare its minimum yearly repayment with step 4. An omitted fact stays `UNKNOWN`, which is not a failed limb.
- The plugin pins `aus-accounting-mcp` 0.12.0, whose `review_div7a_loan` refuses, with no figure, a remaining term longer than the maximum term the Act allows, the loan's own term, or that term less the years elapsed since the loan year. Record the refusal and its code as an exception, check the term against the agreement, and complete step 4 from the agreement's facts.
- If the rates differ, or the engine returns `UNKNOWN`, rely on the ato.gov.au rate and the step 4 calculation, do not use the engine's repayment figures, and record the difference as an exception on the register.

Record what the engine consumed and returned: tool name, package version, inputs, the rate's source and check date from the full response, the comparison with ato.gov.au, and every `UNKNOWN` or `REFUSED` result. Keep the engine's own labels as its own on the register: its shortfall is an experimental review aid, not an assessed deemed dividend. The engine does not classify ITAA 1936 s 109R repayments, UPEs, distributable surplus, interposed entities or debt forgiveness, so steps 2, 5 and 6 stay manual. Without the tools, follow steps 3 and 4 as written.

## Site calculators

Two browser pages on the site can also check step 4 (both read 22 November 2026). The [Division 7A loan calculator](https://duguid.com.au/tools/business-calculators/div7a/) (published 13 November 2026) gives one amalgamated loan's minimum yearly repayment, with an interest and principal split that assumes the whole repayment is made on 30 June, for one income year from the balance and remaining term you enter. The [Division 7A loan schedule calculator](https://duguid.com.au/tools/business-calculators/div7a-schedule/) (published and last checked 17 November 2026) works one complying loan year by year over 7 or 25 years, with each minimum repayment, the interest and any extra repayments. Both take the benchmark rate from the site's rate table, so use them only after step 3, and only where the page's rate for the year matches the ato.gov.au rate. Before relying on either, confirm it supports the reporting month or income year in question: the loan calculator offers the income years 2019-20 to 2026-27, and the schedule takes loans made from 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2027; for any other year, say so and complete step 4 without it. Both run in the browser and send no inputs to a server. Neither replaces the local engine above: use a page as a cross-check only, never as a fallback when a local tool refuses.

Neither decides whether a payment is a loan, whether the agreement complies, whether a shortfall is a deemed dividend or how a distributable surplus limits one, so steps 2, 3, 5 and 6 stay manual. The loan calculator refuses a remaining term outside 1 to 25 years, combines no loans, works no year in which a loan was made and no income year ending on a day other than 30 June. The schedule refuses a loan made before 4 December 1997, a loan made outside 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2027, a loan made through an interposed entity and a repayment above the amount that repays the loan. Enter only amounts, dates and terms, never a shareholder's or company's name. Record what it consumed: the page, the date run, the inputs, the benchmark rate it used and the result. Keep the page's own labels as its own, beside those inputs. Where a page differs from step 4 or refuses, record the difference or refusal as an exception and keep the step 4 figure for the reviewer; a page's figure is a cross-check, never the assessed repayment.

## Checks before handing over

- Benchmark rate cited with ATO source and check date
- Every loan classified, nothing left as 'misc drawings'
- Actions dated against lodgement day, the real deadline for most fixes

## Boundaries

- High-consequence area: this skill produces a register and flags, not conclusions. Deemed-dividend positions, UPE treatment, and repair strategies (for example, converting to complying loans) are reviewer/partner decisions.
- An authorised human decides, documents and carries out any agreement, set-off, repayment, journal, communication or lodgement.
